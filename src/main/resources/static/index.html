🎓 QueuePilot Official Demo Scenario (7 Steps)

Follow the official 7-step presentation flow for course evaluation and professor demonstration.
Step 1: Submit 10 Jobs
Submits 10 tasks (Sorting, Text Analysis, Math, Email) with realistic 10–30s durations and varied priorities (includes J1: P2/10s, J2: P5/30s, J3: P3/5s, J4: P1/2s from the slide).
Step 2: Select Policy
Choose between FCFS (order of arrival), Priority with Aging (prevents starvation), or SJF (shortest job first).
Steps 3 & 4: Execute & Monitor
Watch 4 workers claim 4 jobs simultaneously for 10–30s with live progress bars, while the remaining 6 jobs wait in QUEUED!
Steps 5 & 6: Failure & Recovery
Stop a worker while executing a 20s–30s job. The system detects the expired lease and reassigns the job to another worker.

🎯 One-Click Evaluation Scenarios

Select a scenario below to automatically generate tailored workloads demonstrating core OS scheduling, fault tolerance, and workflow features in real-time.

🌟 Priority & Aging Queue
Queues 6 jobs with priorities 2 to 20. Watch workers claim Critical (20) & Urgent (15) jobs first while low priority jobs age.
⚡ Shortest Job First (SJF)
Queues quick jobs (1.5s, 3s) alongside long jobs (12s, 16s). Shows SJF executing short jobs first to minimize wait time.
🔗 DAG Pipeline (Blocked)
Queues Job A, Job B (depends on A), and Job C (depends on B). Shows BLOCKED status and automated cascading unlocks.
🔄 Auto-Retry & Backoff
Submits a job that triggers simulated runtime failure. Demonstrates retry delays, backoff, and attempt audit history.
⏱️ Deadlines & Overdue SLA
Submits jobs with overdue and near-due deadlines to show real-time ⚠️ OVERDUE and ⏱️ DUE SOON visual indicators.
🕒 Scheduled for Later
Schedules a job 15 seconds in the future. Shows SCHEDULED status which automatically activates when time arrives.
🚫 Cascade Cancellation
Queues a parent job and dependent child, then cancels parent. Shows automatic cascade cancellation of dependent jobs.
🛡️ Worker Lease Expiry
Simulates worker crash and lease expiration on an active job, demonstrating scheduler recovery without job loss.
